EQify 0.3.0 beta: backup, a notification bass button and volume-aware bass.

**Install or update:** in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add `https://github.com/parthsharma365628/eqify-releases`, or download `EQify-0.3.0-beta.apk` below. Your settings are kept when updating.

### New
- **Backup and restore.** Settings → Backup & restore saves your presets, genre curves, device settings and other settings to one file. Restore lets you review the backup first, then Replace or Merge, and you can undo it. Listening history is not included.
- **Bass button in the notification.** Cycle bass boost (Off, +3, +6, +9 dB) without opening the app. You can turn it off in Settings → Sound.
- **Ease bass at high volume.** Optional, in Settings → Sound. Above 60% volume, bass boost fades to half at full volume to avoid distortion. Your bass setting stays the same.

### Notes
- The backup file contains your headphone and device names. It stays on your phone unless you share it.
