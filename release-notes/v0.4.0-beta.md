EQify 0.4.0 beta: sleep timer, widget, loudness compensation, volume limit and more.

**Install or update:** in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add `https://github.com/parthsharma365628/eqify-releases`, or download `EQify-0.4.0-beta.apk` below. Your settings are kept when updating.

### New
- **Sleep timer.** Tap the moon on Home and choose 15–60 minutes or the end of the track. The music fades out over 30 seconds and pauses, then your volume is put back. Touching the volume while it fades cancels it.
- **Home screen widget.** Shows what EQify is doing, with Pause and Bass buttons.
- **Loudness compensation** (Settings → Sound, off by default). Below half volume, it adds a little bass and treble so quiet music still sounds full.
- **Volume limit** (Settings → Sound, off by default). Choose a maximum media volume from 50% to 90%.
- **Test sounds.** Left only, right only, and a 20 Hz–20 kHz sweep, next to the existing low, mid and high tones.
- **Shortcuts and Tasker.** Long-press the app icon for Pause, Bass and Sleep in 30 min. Tasker and similar apps can control EQify once you allow it in Settings → Automation.
- **A short tour** for new users. See it again in Settings → About.
- **Landscape and tablets.** The app now rotates, uses a side rail on wide screens, and puts the EQ sliders beside the controls in landscape.

### Also in this build (from 0.3.0)
- Backup and restore, the notification bass button, and easing bass at high volume.
