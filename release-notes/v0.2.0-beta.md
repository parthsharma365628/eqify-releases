EQify 0.2.0 beta: five new features.

**Install or update:** in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add `https://github.com/parthsharma365628/eqify-releases`, or download `EQify-0.2.0-beta.apk` below. Your settings are kept when updating over 0.1.x.

### New
- **Update check.** EQify tells you on Home when a new version is out. Turn it off in Settings → About.
- **Genre strength.** A slider on Home sets how strongly automatic genre EQ shapes the sound, from subtle to strong.
- **Sound layers.** See what EQify is doing: tone, headphone correction, bass and the total. Find it in EQ → ⋮ → Show sound layers, with a plain-language summary on the Headphones tab.
- **My devices.** Each pair of headphones, the car and the phone speaker can have their own sound: follow genre or always use a preset, plus their own bass and genre strength. Everything starts as "Same as default". Find it in Headphones → My devices.
- **Your week in sound.** Your genres, top artists and busiest time of day, compared with last week. Share it as an image, with artists hidden if you like.

### Notes
- Stats count songs EQify detected, not listening time.
- Update checks ask GitHub for the latest version once a day; see the privacy policy.
