EQify 0.5.0 beta: safe listening, a live spectrum, a Wear OS remote and more.

**Install or update:** in [Obtainium](https://github.com/ImranR98/Obtainium), add `https://github.com/parthsharma365628/eqify-releases`, or download `EQify-0.5.0-beta.apk` below. Your settings are kept when updating.

### New
- **Safe listening** (Settings → Safe listening, on by default).
  - EQify estimates how much of the WHO weekly listening allowance you've used: 80 dB for 40 hours for adults, or 75 dB in sensitive mode.
  - It counts only while music plays on headphones.
  - It reminds you at 100%, with options to lower the volume or turn on the volume limit.
  - It's an estimate based on your volume, not a measurement.
- **Live spectrum** (Home). See the frequencies of what's playing. It asks for "Record audio", which Android requires to read EQify's own output. The microphone is never used.
- **AutoEQ ParametricEQ import.** Headphones → ⋮ → Import now also accepts `ParametricEQ.txt` files.
- **Dialogue preset**, for clearer voices in films and videos.
- **Match loudness** on the EQ screen, so EQ and Original compare at the same level.
- **Stereo balance** (Settings → Sound), for uneven hearing or a quieter earbud. Needs Balanced output protection.
- **Favourite presets.** Star presets to pin them to the top of the list, in your own order.
- **Slider ticks.** A light vibration per dB while dragging the EQ sliders.
- **What changed** (Home). A list of the automatic changes EQify made since it started.
- **Remember volume per device** (Settings → Sound, off by default).
- **New quick-settings tiles** for Bass and the Sleep timer.
- **Wear OS remote** (optional): download `EQify-Wear-0.5.0-beta.apk` and see the README for installing it on a watch.
